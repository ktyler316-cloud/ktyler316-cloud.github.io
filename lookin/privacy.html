Privacy Policy

Look In: Watch Camera · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing, because nothing leaves your devices.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no user accounts, and no servers of its own. Nothing is recorded or saved: the picture and sound exist only while you are looking in, and only on your own iPhone and Apple Watch.

What the app sends over the network

Nothing goes to the internet. While you look in, the picture and sound travel directly from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch over your own Wi‑Fi network, signed and encrypted with a key that only those two devices have. Pairing details travel between them through Apple's WatchConnectivity.

Camera, microphone and local network

The iPhone's camera and microphone are used only to show and play the room on your watch while you look in. The watch's microphone is used only for Hold to Talk, which plays your voice out of the iPhone's speaker. Local network access is used only to reach your own watch. Nothing is recorded, stored, or shared with anyone.

Purchases

When you buy the app, Apple handles the purchase under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data. There is nothing to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
