Privacy Policy

Tenacious: Reminders · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing, because nothing leaves your devices.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no user accounts, and no servers of its own. Your reminders, their history, and your settings are stored only on your iPhone and, if you use it, your paired Apple Watch.

What the app sends over the network

Nothing. Reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on the device. When you use the Apple Watch app, your reminders are synced between your iPhone and your paired watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity, directly between your own devices.

Notifications

Notification permission is used only to alert you to your own reminders. Notifications are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Purchases

When you buy the app, Apple handles the purchase under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data. There is nothing to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
